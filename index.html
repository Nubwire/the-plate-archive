Our Story

The Plate Archive Co. is built on the belief that beauty endures. We scour estates, forgotten cellars, and specialized auctions across the globe to curate an impeccable collection of rare, vintage, and antique ceramic plates.

Each piece in our archive carries a history—a silent observer to centuries of shared meals, fervent conversations, and quiet mornings. We are proud to be a Federation Collectibles Company, adhering to the highest standards of preservation and provenance.

Shipping & Authenticity

Global Secure Delivery

We provide fully insured, worldwide shipping for all our curated pieces. Understanding the fragile and irreplaceable nature of antique ceramics, our archivists utilize custom foam-in-place technology, double-boxing, and reinforced structural packing to guarantee absolute safety during transit.

Processing Time: All orders are carefully processed and dispatched from our primary archive within 1-3 business days. You will receive a tracking manifest via email once your acquisition is en route.

Authenticity Guarantee: The Plate Archive Co. unconditionally guarantees the authenticity of every item sold. Each piece is meticulously inspected by our specialists for maker's marks, glaze consistency, crazing patterns, and era-appropriate characteristics before being accepted into the archive.

Privacy Policy

Information Security

The Plate Archive Co. values the privacy and anonymity of our collectors. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict guidelines regarding your personal data.

1. Data Collection: We collect only the information necessary to process your acquisitions, maintain your curation preferences, and communicate shipping details. This includes your name, shipping address, email, and secure payment tokens (which we do not store on our own servers).

2. Information Usage: Your data is used exclusively for internal operations. We do not sell, rent, or distribute our client manifest to any third-party entities. Period.

3. Communication: By providing your email, you consent to receive correspondence regarding your specific inquiries or orders. Marketing communications are strictly opt-in.

Terms of Service

Conditions of Acquisition

By engaging with The Plate Archive Co. and acquiring our vintage pieces, you agree to the following conditions:

1. Nature of Vintage Goods: You are purchasing objects of history. Natural wear, minor gilding loss, slight utensil marks, or crazing are testaments to age and use, not defects. We photograph and describe all condition issues to the best of our ability. Perfection should not be expected in wares that have survived a century or more.

2. Returns & Refunds: Due to the logistical risk of transporting fragile antiques, all sales are considered final upon dispatch. Returns are only authorized if an item significantly deviates from its archived description, or is damaged in transit (which must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence of the packaging and item).

3. Intellectual Property: All imagery, text, and curation frameworks on this site are the intellectual property of The Plate Archive Co., a Federation Collectibles Company. Unauthorized reproduction is prohibited.
