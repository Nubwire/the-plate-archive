Our Story

The Plate Archive Co. is built on the belief that beauty endures. We scour estates, forgotten cellars, and specialised auctions across the globe to curate an impeccable collection of rare, vintage, and antique ceramic plates.

Each piece in our archive carries a history — a silent observer to centuries of shared meals, fervent conversations, and quiet mornings. We are proud to be a Federation Collectibles Company, adhering to the highest standards of preservation and provenance.

Shipping & Authenticity

Global Secure Delivery

We provide fully insured, worldwide shipping for all our curated pieces. Our archivists utilise custom foam-in-place technology, double-boxing, and reinforced structural packing to guarantee absolute safety during transit.

Processing Time: All orders are carefully processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days. You will receive a tracking manifest via email once your acquisition is en route.

Authenticity Guarantee: Every item sold is inspected for maker's marks, glaze consistency, crazing patterns, and era-appropriate characteristics before being accepted into the archive.

Privacy Policy

Information Security

We collect only the information necessary to process your acquisitions and communicate shipping details. Your data is never sold or distributed to third parties.

Communication: By providing your email, you consent to receive correspondence regarding specific inquiries or orders. Marketing communications are strictly opt-in.

Conditions of Acquisition

You are purchasing objects of history. Natural wear, minor gilding loss, or crazing are testaments to age, not defects.

Returns & Refunds: All sales are considered final upon dispatch. Returns are only authorised if an item significantly deviates from its archived description, or is damaged in transit (reported within 48 hours with photographic evidence).
